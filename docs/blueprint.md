# **App Name**: Human Rights Affairs Trust (JusticeShield)

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Legal Assistant: An intelligent chatbot tool that uses reasoning to help users understand their constitutional rights and legal procedures through conversational interaction.
- Chairman’s Leadership Hub: A dedicated high-impact section featuring a formal message from the Chairman accompanied by professional photography to build organizational trust.
- Member Identity Showcase: Visual display of sample digital Member Identity Cards, showcasing QR-code verification and security features to encourage membership enrollment.
- Digital Membership & QR Verification: Full registration system with secure data storage in Firestore, providing members with verifiable digital IDs.
- Multilingual Knowledge Center: A digital library of rights guides and legal manuals supporting English, Hindi, and Bengali, with PDF storage in Firebase Storage.
- Emergency Helpline Dashboard: Interactive, click-to-call emergency cards for national and local helplines with state-wise filtering.
- AI-Enhanced Document Search: An advanced search tool that helps users quickly find specific legal clauses or human rights documentation within the extensive site library.
- Secure Donation & Receipting: Integrated payment system for donations that automatically generates tax-exempt receipts and tracks fund usage.

## Style Guidelines:

- A vibrant and colorful palette blending professional Navy Blue (#1A365D) with energetic Saffron (#FF9933) and Emerald Green (#138808) accents, set against a crisp white background.
- Extensive use of rich, cinematic Framer Motion animations, including staggered entrance effects, smooth parallax scrolling, and interactive hover-states for cards.
- A modern, attractive interface utilizing glassmorphism effects, bento-grid modules for the Knowledge Center, and a dedicated spotlight layout for the Chairman's Message.
- Dignified 'Playfair Display' for headlines to convey authority, paired with 'Inter' for highly readable and modern body text across all legal documentation.
- Vibrant, multi-colored duo-tone icons that represent social justice, legal aid, and humanitarian themes with high visual appeal.
